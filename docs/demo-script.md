# Demo script — 75 seconds

Recorded on a phone, portrait, in the MiniPay in-app browser if possible.
Every result below is real and reproducible; nothing is staged.

Record in one take if you can. A slightly rough single take reads as honest;
a polished cut reads as marketing.

---

**0:00–0:08 — The problem, in the user's words**

*On screen:* the app open, empty input, "Is this safe to sign?" visible.

> "Someone sends you a coin. Or a link. You have no idea if it's real, and if
> you're wrong the money's gone."

---

**0:08–0:22 — A real scam**

*On screen:* tap **A real scam**. The result comes back red.

> "This one's on a public list of addresses that have already stolen from
> people. It says: do not sign. Not 'risky' — do not sign."

*Point at the reasons.* Let the red verdict word and the one-line reason sit on
screen for a beat.

---

**0:22–0:34 — Warning someone else**

*On screen:* tap **Warn someone**, show the WhatsApp share sheet.

> "And here's the part that matters. Scams arrive in WhatsApp groups, so the
> warning goes back into the same group — with a link so the next person can
> check for themselves."

*Do not send it.* Show the sheet and dismiss.

---

**0:34–0:50 — The honest middle**

*On screen:* paste cUSD — `0x765DE816845861e75A25fCA122bb6898B8B1282a`.
Result: **What this can do**, citing the upgradeable proxy.

> "Now the cUSD in your MiniPay wallet. It doesn't say scam — it says what this
> can do. The code behind it can be changed later. That's normal for a big
> stablecoin, and you should still know it."

*Expand **What we checked*** to show the four questions with three passes.

> "It shows what it checked, not just what it found."

---

**0:50–1:03 — What it refuses to answer**

*On screen:* paste a Stellar address (`C…`) or a Dexscreener pair link.

> "And when it can't check something, it says so instead of guessing. No green
> tick, no red screen — just: this isn't something I can check."

*This beat matters more than it looks.* It is the difference between a safety
tool and a toy.

---

**1:03–1:15 — Close**

*On screen:* back to the empty input.

> "No wallet connection, ever. It never asks to touch your money. Works on a
> cheap phone on slow data. It's for the 14 million people on MiniPay who are
> one wrong tap from losing a month's income."

---

## Before recording

- [ ] Deployed URL, opened in MiniPay on a real handset
- [ ] Clear the history list, or accept that it shows (it is honest either way)
- [ ] Phone on Do Not Disturb; hide notifications
- [ ] Have the cUSD address and the Stellar address ready to paste
- [ ] Check both example buttons return what this script says they do

## Do not

- Do not speed up the video to hide a slow check. If it is slow on a real
  connection, that is worth knowing.
- Do not stage a result the tool did not produce.
